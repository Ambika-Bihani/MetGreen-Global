About Company

MetGreen Global

MetGreen Global is a trusted partner in the global recycling and commodity trade industry. We work alongside processors, manufacturers, mills, smelters, recycling yards, and logistics partners to ensure a seamless flow of recyclable materials across international borders.

Our core trading categories include ferrous and non-ferrous metals, recyclable rubber, and recovered paper. Each is handled with precision, verified documentation, and strict grade integrity from source to destination.

We operate with a strong emphasis on quality assurance, transparent communication, and long-term business relationships. Whether you require a one-time shipment or ongoing supply, our team delivers consistent, dependable service tailored to your trade requirements.
